Case study 01 · Full product cycle

Qingzhou Music Festival
踏音希生，让渡音命

A student-led live music festival developed and delivered by a small university team at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University.

01 / Product proposition

Defined a campus-scale live music experience around open-air performance, student participation and cross-community connection, with a clear audience proposition and three-act programme.

02 / Product design

Translated the idea into artist research, lineup development, partner and sponsorship planning, publicity routes, ticketing, venue zoning, staffing and audience movement.

03 / Product delivery

Coordinated artist liaison and on-site arrangements while participating across the wider operating system: stage, volunteers, security, schedules, materials and show-day control.
