Case study 01 · Full product cycle

Qingzhou Music Festival
踏音希生，让渡音命

A student-led festival co-organised by a small university team at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University and delivered for an audience of more than 3,000.

01 / Audience proposition

Shaped an open-air campus festival around student participation and cross-community connection, with a three-act programme designed to bring different music audiences into one shared event.

02 / Planning system

Connected artist and band profiles with promotion, sponsorship, ticket registration, venue zoning, staffing and audience movement so that creative choices remained workable.

03 / Show-day system

Aligned artist requirements, soundchecks and backstage arrangements with volunteers, security, audience flow and contingency response. Each operational decision affected the conditions in which performers could focus on the music.

3 live acts 3,000+ actual audience 45+ safety and event personnel planned 1 student organising team
Working evidence: festival proposal · artist and band profiles · sponsorship and partner plans · master timeline · division of responsibilities · budget framework · venue map · safety and emergency plan · promotional materials

Case study 02

Holding the energy:
Montreux Music Carnival

Context

A 2,000+ attendee carnival delivered by the company team, combining an international lineup with a complex live environment and multiple artist movements.

My practice

Managed communication with all participating artists, aligning schedules, technical requirements and venue readiness from arrival and soundcheck through performance.

Operating principle

Artist support depended on details staying connected: a timing change could alter transport, backstage readiness, technical handovers and the next stage movement.

Working evidence: venue and stage photography · artist liaison · programme coordination · live delivery
