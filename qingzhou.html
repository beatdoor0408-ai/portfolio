Case study 03 · Independent organising

Qingzhou Music Festival
踏音希生，让渡音命

A student-led live music festival independently planned and delivered by Haoran Wang and a fellow organiser at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University.

Project profile

September 2023 · XJTLU campus stadium · approximately 2,000-person audience target · three live performance acts.

From plan to delivery

Programming, artist and band liaison, venue and stage coordination, ticketing, promotion, volunteer organisation, security planning and show-day operations.

Why it matters

This project demonstrates independent ownership: translating a concept into a working event system with people, time, space, safety and audience experience held together.

The organiser’s view

The project moved through concept development, artist and venue coordination, promotion, ticketing, volunteer management, safety planning and final delivery. The full timeline is an important part of the story: it shows how an idea becomes a live event.
